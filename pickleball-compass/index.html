Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Designed for real pickleball players — fast to log, smart to analyze.

🏓

Fast Score Logging

Record a game in seconds. Enter four players, scores, and you're done. Or log a session — all your games at one location on one day — without a score, and fill it in later. Log from your iPhone or Apple Watch, and share results with others.

📊

Player Stats & Streaks

See win rates, records, and trends for yourself and every player you've faced. Track win streaks, days played streaks, and milestone wins.

👋

Never Forget a Name

Met four new people at open play? Jot a quick hint — "lefty, red visor, teacher" — and the app remembers everyone you've ever played, sorted by who you saw last. Next Tuesday, greet Mike like an old friend.

⌚

Apple Watch App

Log games right from your wrist without pulling out your phone. Stats sync instantly between your iPhone and Apple Watch.

❤️

Health Integration

Optionally pull in heart rate from your Apple Watch pickleball workout, plus sleep from the night before, to see how your body performs on big days.

📅

Schedule Calendar

See your pickleball calendar events alongside your recorded games — color-coded so you always know what's coming and what's been played.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Get a reminder before your game, a nudge when it's time to play, a prompt to log your session afterward, and a Monday morning recap of your week — every alert is optional and toggles individually.

🪪

Player Profile

Build a profile with your contact info, gear details, and favorite places to play — share it with people you meet, complete with a contact card they can add straight to their Contacts.

🏸

Paddles & Balls Guide

Browse top paddle brands and models with type and level tags, plus the leading ball brands — with direct links to each company's site.

📖

Rules Reference

Full pickleball rules reference built right in — serving, kitchen, faults, scoring, and more. Includes a link to the official USA Pickleball rulebook.

🎓

Training Videos

Curated beginner videos covering rules, serving, kitchen play, and common mistakes — all watchable without leaving the app.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Games, players, leagues, and settings stay in sync across your devices automatically — through your own private iCloud, never our servers. No account required.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export your game history to JSON or CSV and import backups — perfect for switching devices or keeping an archive.

🏆

Leagues

Great for a group you play with regularly. Create leagues — set a fixed number of weeks or leave them ongoing. Track weekly results, see who you've played with and against, and archive a league once the season wraps.
