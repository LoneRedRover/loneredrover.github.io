If the link brings you back to this page with the app installed, use the button above.

Download on the App Store

What the link holds

The games, who played, where, and the name of the card. It does not hold the sender's notes or any rack photos. The games travel inside the link itself, in the part your browser never sends to a website, so this page has not seen them.
