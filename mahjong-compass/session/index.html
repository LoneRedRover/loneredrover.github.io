Mahjong Compass

Someone sent you a night of mahjong

One person at the table recorded the games in Mahjong Compass and sent them to you, so you do not have to write them up again. The link you tapped carries the whole night.

If you do not have the app yet

Get Mahjong Compass from the App Store, then go back to the message and tap the link again. The games open in the app, you say who is who, and they join your own record. Receiving a night is free.

Download on the App Store

If you have the app and landed here anyway

Go back to the message, touch and hold the link, and choose Open in Mahjong Compass. A newly installed app can take a little while to start answering its links.

The link only opens the app on an iPhone or iPad. On a Mac or a PC it brings you to this page.

What the link holds

The games, who played, where, and the name of the card. It does not hold the sender's notes or any rack photos. The games travel inside the link itself, in the part your browser never sends to a website, so this page has not seen them.
