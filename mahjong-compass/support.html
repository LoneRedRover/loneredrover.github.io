Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record a hand?

Tap the + button and fill in who won and which line they won on. Everything else — the money, dead hands, the timer — is optional. Tap Save and the hand joins the night.

Do I have to name everyone at the table?

No. Seats you leave blank become stand-ins — "Player 2", "Player 3" — and a hand records perfectly well against them. Stand-ins stay out of your people list, so a stranger in the fourth seat never turns into someone you appear to play with regularly. Name them later and the record follows.

Why doesn't the app know this year's card?

The card belongs to the National Mah Jongg League, and Mahjong Compass ships none of it. Instead you set up this year's section headings once, in whatever shorthand your table uses, and the picker stores a coordinate — section, line, and which variation. Buy the card from the League; this is the notebook you keep beside it.

How do I set up a new card year?

Go to Settings → Cards, add the new year's card, and enter its section headings and how many lines each one has. Your previous years stay exactly as they were, so old games keep pointing at the card they were actually played on.

Where do hand values come from?

You enter a hand's value the first time you win on a line, and the app remembers it for that line from then on. It is your copy of your card, built up as you play — nothing is shipped with the app and nothing is sent anywhere.

How does the settlement work?

Give the app the hand value, the winner, and whether the hand was self-drawn or thrown — and by whom. It applies the common convention: everyone pays face value, the player who threw the winning tile pays double, a self-drawn win doubles everyone, and a jokerless hand is worth double. You can see the result as every pair who owe each other, or as the fewest payments that clear the night, in Settings → Settling Up.

Can I hide the money?

Yes. Turn off Show Money in Games in Settings and the figures disappear from the games list. Nothing stops being recorded — the settlement is still there whenever you want it.

Why can't I edit an old hand?

Hands lock themselves a day after they're played, so a mis-tap on months of history can't quietly rewrite it. Tap the padlock on a sitting to open that whole day for an hour, then it closes itself again. You can also lock tonight's sitting early, the moment it's done.

What is the timer for?

Tournament play runs on a clock, and timing a hand at your own table is how you find out whether you're ready for one. It's entirely optional. Saving a hand doesn't stop the clock, a new game starts it at zero, and it stops itself after two hours if you walk away.

Will my data sync across my devices?

Yes. Your games, people, and places sync automatically through your own private iCloud account — sign in to iCloud on a new device and your record appears there. Nothing is stored on our servers, and no separate account is needed. You can also export everything to JSON or CSV at any time if you'd like your own backup.

The calendar isn't finding my game nights.

The app reads your calendar with your permission and matches the spellings people actually use — mahjong, mah jong, mahjongg, mah jongg, mahj. If nothing appears, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars and make sure Mahjong Compass has access. The app only ever reads; it never creates or changes anything in your calendar.
