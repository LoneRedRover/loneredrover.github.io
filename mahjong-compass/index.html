A record-keeping app

Mahjong Compass is not a game you play against the phone. It is the notebook for the game you play with three other people at a real table — the one that usually ends up as scribbles on the back of an envelope.

Features

Fast between hands.
Honest at the end of the night.

If writing a hand down takes longer than dealing the next one, nobody writes it down. Everything here is built around that.

🀄

Log a Hand in Seconds

Who won, which line, concealed or exposed, jokerless — recorded between hands while the tiles are still out. Or log the sitting first and fill the hands in afterward, when the evening is over.

💵

Settle Up

Give it the winner, the line and who threw the tile — the app works out the rest. The value comes off the line. Doubling for the discarder, doubling on a self-drawn win, jokerless hands. A night settles in the fewest payments that clear it, and every hand shows what it cost on its own.

🃏

Your Card, Your Shorthand

The app records a coordinate — section, line, variant — and what that line is worth. Any label you put on a line is your own: it stays on your device and never appears in a share.

💀

Dead Hands and Wall Games

Every player at the table gets an outcome, not just the winner. Mark a dead hand with a tap, record a wall game when the tiles run out, and see how often it happens — to you and to everyone else.

📊

Stats That Mean Something

Wins by line and what's still unwon on this year's card. Win rate overall and by place. Dead-hand frequency. And money over time, per player and per location — the nemesis stat.

📍

Where You Play

Every sitting carries its location, so you can see how you do at Gail's kitchen table versus the club. It fills in the place you're most likely at without being asked twice.

⏱️

Hand Timer

Tournament play runs on a clock. Time a hand at your own table and find out whether you're ready for one. Optional, and it keeps running while you write the hand up.

🤝

Adjustments

Money that moves by agreement rather than by the hand — a mahjong declared in error, a side bet, an old debt. Recorded against the hand it happened in, in plain words.

📅

Your Mahjong Calendar

The nights you played and the dates already in your own calendar, in one month at a time. It finds them however you spell it — mahjong, mah jongg, mahj.

👋

Everyone You Play With

The people at your table, remembered — with your record against each of them. Nobody has to be named to record a hand, so a stranger at the fourth seat never stops you writing the night down.

📝

Session Notes

One note per sitting, for the things a scoreboard can't hold. What went wrong, what someone taught you, what you want to try next week.

🔒

History That Stays Put

Hands close themselves a day after they're played, so a stray tap on months of history can't quietly rewrite it. Lock a night early when it's done, and unlock a day for an hour when you genuinely need to fix something.

📤

Share the Night

Send the table a clean summary — every hand, who won on what, and the payments that settle it. Your own private shorthand never leaves your phone.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Your games, people, and places stay in sync across your devices automatically — through your own private iCloud, never our servers. No account to create.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export everything to JSON or CSV and import it back. Your record is yours, and you can take it out whenever you want.

About the card

You still need the card.

Mahjong Compass knows enough about the 2026 National Mah Jongg League card to record what you played — the section names, how many lines are in each, which lines print two hands, which have to be played concealed, and what each one is worth.

It does not reproduce the card. The hands themselves, the tile combinations you are actually building, are not in the app and will not be. You cannot play from your phone, and nothing here is a substitute for the card on the table.

To play American Mah Jongg you need the League's card. Buy it from the National Mah Jongg League.

Anything you type yourself — your own shorthand for a line, a note about a night — stays on your device. It is never sent to us and never appears in anything you share.

Next year's card will arrive in an app update, and Chinese Mah Jongg after that. None of them replaces the card in front of you.
